Technical Services Division

The Technical Division of the Grenada Bureau of Standards offers a wide range of services to support the National Quality Infrastructure. The Technical Services Division encompasses all the services offered by the various laboratories of Bureau' and its conformity assessment programmes for existing technical regulations and voluntary standards.

Label Certification

Producers and manufacturers have your labels certified today.
Labels can be submitted to the GDBS via email address conformity@gdbs.gd or hard copies can be submitted to our office at Queen’s Park, St George’s. The cost of certification is $15 per label.

The Grenada Bureau of Standards has established several conformity assessment programmes for the technical regulations under its portfolio. Conformity assessment involves the inspection of goods imported and produced for the local market, testing and certification activities.
