Microbiology

The Grenada Bureau of Standards Microbiology Laboratory provides a range of testing services geared at ensuring food and food derived products produced locally and imported are safe. The team of highly trained and experienced professionals is committed to producing accurate, reliable and timely results. To assure this commitment the laboratory uses strict quality control practices and methodologies.
