DGDS 14:202X – Specification for Toilet Tissue

DGDS 14:202X – Specification for Toilet Tissue is now open for public review.
This draft covers single-ply and two-ply toilet tissue made from virgin, recycled, or mixed fibre. It does not apply to facial tissue or paper towels and will replace GDS 14:1992.

This draft will be mandatory once approved.

Submit your comments via email to standardization@gdbs.gd
