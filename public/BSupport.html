BUSINESS SUPPORT

Training

The Grenada Bureau of Standards also provides training and offer technical guidance to help your business grow. For further information contact us via email address gdbs@gdbs.gd

Packaging Materials

To support Grenada's Argo-processing sector the Grenada Bureau of Standards provides a small line of packaging materials inclusive of bottles, pouches and PPE bags. For more information contact us via email address gdbs@gdbs.gd

Barcode Generation

This service involves the generation of barcodes using International Guidelines (GS1) and uses the EAN/UPC symbology which is the most recognizable family of barcodes in the GS1 System. It was developed as part of the Agro-processing project "Livelihood Development via Agro-processing" funded under the European Union’s (EU) Special Framework of Assistance for 2009 (SFA 2009) through the Food and Agriculture Organization (FAO).

WHAT IS REQUIRED
1. Completed application form
2. Copy of an approved label that conforms to the Grenada National Standards for labelling (GDS 1: Part 1: 2021, GDS 1: Part 2: 2021, GDS 1: Part 4: 2018)

For further information contact us at info@gdbs.gd

Label Certification

The Grenada Bureau of Standards (GDBS) label certification programme is based on requirements of mandatory standards GDS 1: Part 1: 2021, GDS 1: Part 2: 2021, GDS 1: Part 4: 2018. To ensure compliance with the labelling regulations the Technical Services Division of the GDBS offers a certification service which examines your labels for compliance.

WHAT IS REQUIRED
1. A completed Application for Approval of Labels for your product(s)
(Application for Approval of Labels Form)
2. A sample of the product label should be submitted via email to conformity@gdbs.gd or delivered in person.
