Technical Regulations

Technical regulations and standards are a set of rules and guidelines that define the characteristics of a product, including its size, shape, design, functions, performance, labeling, packaging, and more. Technical regulations (Compulsory Standards) are mandatory and are put in place to ensure the safety, quality, and environmental impact of products.
